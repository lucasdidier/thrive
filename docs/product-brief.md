# Thrive · Product brief v0.1

Status: working hypotheses for founder review; customer research has not yet been conducted.

## Customer and promise

Start with independent designers and consultants selling fixed-fee projects with a deposit and delivery milestones. “Describe how your business works. Thrive sets up a simple workspace that takes clients from inquiry to payment.”

## Core journey

Inquiry → contact and engagement → discovery call → proposal/agreement → deposit invoice → payment → delivery phases → final invoice.

## Initial boundaries

One business owner per workspace; one billing currency per engagement; a first launch market still to be selected. Production calendar ecosystem, signature provider, and invoice requirements remain open decisions. No full accounting, arbitrary generated code, agency resource planning, or general website builder in the initial milestone.

## Interface principles

- Quiet surfaces, restrained color, strong typography, generous spacing.
- Shared list/detail/form patterns across objects.
- Client context follows the work; never re-enter client details for each feature.
- Every automation is inspectable, editable, and explainable.
- Progressive disclosure: show useful work first and configuration when needed.
- Maintain keyboard access, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.

## Prototype acceptance

- Navigate an overview, clients, projects, invoices, and workflows.
- Create a client and related project; filter/search existing records.
- Inspect phases, track time manually, and review a proposal.
- Simulate proposal acceptance and create one deposit invoice only.
- Record external payments in full or in part, with dates and references, and update connected project/invoice summaries.
- Describe a simple setup, review an explicitly rule-based preview, edit the deposit and phases, and apply it to future projects.
- Switch dark/light appearance and retain local changes after reload.

## Research to do

Interview 10–15 prospective users and recruit 5–8 pilot partners. Collect anonymized recent project journeys, documents, copying between tools, billing exceptions, switching reasons, and actual software spend. Test whether the connected journey and understandable customization are enough to motivate switching.

## Proposed success measures

Useful setup in under 15 minutes; first real client action without assistance; repeat use on a second engagement; understandable automation behavior; sustainable support effort. Targets are hypotheses, not observed results.
