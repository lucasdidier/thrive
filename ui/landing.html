Questions

Something else? Write to hello@thrive.example.

Who is Thrive for?

Freelancers and small studios who sell projects, packages or hourly work: designers, developers, consultants, photographers, copywriters and coaches. If you need payroll, inventory or a large team, Thrive isn’t built for that yet.

Is my data safe?

Your workspace is private to your account and backed up every night. You can ask for a full copy at any time. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Is it really free?

Yes, for the first 100 people we let in, with no credit card. If we ever introduce paid plans, we’ll tell you at least 30 days ahead, and you can take a copy of your data with you.

Can I automate my own follow-ups?

Yes. A workflow pairs a trigger, such as “Proposal accepted”, with the actions that should follow. There are 77 triggers and 43 actions, and one workflow can chain up to 20 actions. Build them in the editor, start from a template, or ask your AI assistant.

Which AI assistants work with it?

Any assistant that supports MCP, including Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and Codex. You add one address and sign in. Agents can read your records, draft emails and build workflows, and they preview changes before saving them.

Do I need Gmail or Google Calendar?

You sign in with a Google account. Connecting Gmail, Outlook or a calendar is optional, and unlocks client emails, meeting details and booking pages.

Can clients pay online?

Not yet. Thrive issues invoices as links with your payment details, and tracks payments as you record them. Card payments are on the roadmap.
