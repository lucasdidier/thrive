Questions

Ask us at hello@thrive.example.

Who is Thrive for?

Freelancers and small studios who sell projects, packages or hours. It isn’t built for payroll, inventory or large teams.

How much does it cost?

Thrive Cloud is $10 a month after a 7-day free trial. Hosting it yourself is free. Every feature is in both.

Can I host it myself?

Yes. Thrive is open source under the MIT licence, and the code and setup steps are on GitHub.

Is it ready for real client work?

Yes, it’s used with real clients. It’s still a beta: clients can’t pay online yet, and signatures are typed names with a timestamp.

Is my data safe?

Your workspace is private and backed up every night. Lists export to CSV, and you can ask for a full copy anytime. See the Privacy Policy.

Which AI assistants work with it?

Any that supports MCP, including Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex and Copilot. Changes are previewed before they’re saved.

Do I need a Google account?

No. Sign in with Google, or with a one-time code we send to your email.
