Questions

Who is Thrive for?

Independent service businesses: freelancers and small studios who sell projects, packages or hourly work. Designers, developers, consultants, photographers, copywriters and coaches all fit well. If you need payroll, inventory or a large team, Thrive isn’t built for that yet.

Is it really free?

Yes, for the first 100 people we let in. You don’t need a credit card. If we ever introduce paid plans, we’ll tell you at least 30 days ahead, and you can take your data with you.

Which AI assistants work with Thrive?

Any assistant that supports MCP, including Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and Codex. You paste one URL and sign in. Agents act with your access, preview changes first, and never send email or calendar invites without your explicit OK.

Can I create my own automations?

Yes. Each workflow pairs one of 77 triggers with one of 43 actions, like “Proposal accepted → Create contract draft”. Build them in the editor, start from a template, or ask your AI assistant to build and test them for you.

Do I need Gmail or Google Calendar?

You sign in with a Google account. Connecting Gmail, Outlook or a calendar is optional, and unlocks client emails, meeting details and booking pages.

Can clients pay online?

Not yet. Thrive creates your invoices, shares them as a link with your payment details, and tracks payments as you record them. Online card payments are on the roadmap.

Is my data safe?

Your workspace is private to your account and backed up every night. You can ask us for a full copy at any time. Read our Privacy Policy for the details.
