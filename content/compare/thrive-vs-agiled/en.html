Choose Agiled if

  • You want clients to pay by card inside the app, through Stripe, PayPal or Mollie.
  • You need recurring invoices, automatic payment reminders, deposits and payment schedules.
  • You run a team and want tasks, Gantt views, support tickets and HR tools in the same place.
  • You want phone apps, drawn signatures, or a client portal with logins on your own domain.

Choose Thrive if

  • You're a freelancer or a small studio, and your clients pay by bank transfer or your own payment link.
  • You want proposals, contracts, invoices, time and client email working together without a long setup.
  • You want one plan with everything included, automations too, for $10 a month or $100 a year.
  • You want open-source software you can read and self-host.

Feature by feature

Clients and pipeline

Agiled's CRM covers contacts, accounts (companies), deal pipelines, forms and support tickets. Starter allows 500 contacts and one pipeline; Pro has unlimited contacts and three pipelines.

Thrive's client list shows pipeline stages, and clients, projects, documents and invoices can be viewed as Kanban boards. There's no limit on how many clients you work with.

Agiled goes further for sales teams. For a freelancer's client list, both do the job.

Proposals and contracts

In Agiled, proposals and contracts live under Docs. You start from templates and place a signature block for each signer. Clients sign from a link with no account, and choose to draw their signature or type their full name. Smart Flows put a proposal, contract, deposit and booking on one guided page, and can offer packages and add-ons. Starter allows 25 docs; Pro and up are unlimited.

Thrive proposals start from eight editable templates. You can include all services, let the client choose one package, or choose several, with quantities they can adjust. If project billing is on, the deposit invoice is issued as soon as the client accepts or signs. Contracts are signed with a typed name, by one signer, with no third-party e-signature service. See how to offer packages in proposals.

Agiled is stronger for signatures, especially with several signers. Both let clients choose packages.

Invoices and payments

Agiled connects to Stripe, PayPal and Mollie, so clients can pay invoices by card from a link, with no login. Its help center describes recurring invoices that can charge a saved card, payment reminders, deposits and payment schedules, late fees, multi-currency invoices and expenses. QuickBooks or Xero sync is listed from the Business plan.

Thrive doesn't process payments. Your invoices show the payment instructions you write, such as bank details or your own payment link. Clients pay outside Thrive and you record the payment. There are no recurring invoices and no automatic payment reminders.

Agiled is clearly stronger for getting paid.

Scheduling

Agiled has booking pages and event types, with Google, Outlook and Apple calendars, buffers, minimum notice, Google Meet or Zoom links, and paid bookings through its payment providers. Team scheduling is listed from Pro.

Thrive has booking pages that check your connected Google or Microsoft calendar, with buffers and notice times. Paid bookings aren't possible.

Agiled does more, especially if you charge for sessions or share bookings across a team.

Email

Agiled connects Gmail, Outlook or any IMAP/SMTP mailbox, so you can email clients from Agiled and see each conversation on their record. Its pricing page lists email from the Pro plan.

Thrive connects your own Gmail or Outlook account. Conversations are linked to the client and project, and you reply from Thrive.

Both handle everyday client email well.

Automations

Agiled's workflows use a visual builder with one starting point, such as a submitted form, and steps that can wait, branch into paths, ask your team for approval or repeat for each item. Workflows can also run on a schedule. They start on the Pro plan, with 10 automations and 1,000 runs a month; Business allows 50 and Scale 250.

Thrive workflows pair one event with up to 20 actions: "when a proposal is accepted, create the contract and draft a welcome email." They're included in the one plan, with no run limits listed. They react to events only, not to dates.

Agiled's automations go deeper. Thrive's are included from day one and easier to read later.

Client portal

Every Agiled plan includes a client portal where clients sign in with an emailed code, or with Google or a password if you turn them on. They see invoices, projects, tickets, documents, files and chat. Your brand and a custom domain are included on Business and Scale, or a $19 monthly add-on on Starter and Pro.

Thrive's client portal is a private link, with no login, showing the documents, forms, invoices, meetings and project status you choose to share. There are no client accounts, no portal messaging and no custom domain.

Agiled is stronger if clients need to log in, chat or open tickets.

Projects, tasks and time

Agiled includes projects with tasks, milestones and Kanban, list and Gantt views, plus time tracking on every plan. Pro and up add HR tools.

Thrive projects hold documents, invoices, time and messages, with Kanban boards for records but no task lists. Time entries by project turn into invoice drafts. Thrive doesn't track expenses.

Agiled is stronger if you manage tasks and a team in the same tool.

AI, mobile and your data

Agiled includes AI credits and AI workers on every plan, and connects to ChatGPT or Claude through its MCP server to look up records and prepare invoice drafts. It has apps for phones and computers, and lets you set the language clients see.

Thrive is also an MCP server. Claude or ChatGPT can look up clients, prepare proposals or invoices, and draft emails, with nothing sent until you approve. The app is available in 10 languages and runs in the browser, with no mobile app. Thrive is open source under the MIT license, so you can read the code or self-host it on your own server.

Both work with AI assistants. Agiled has phone apps; Thrive is the one you can self-host.

Pricing

  • Pro: $59 a month billed annually, or $69 billed monthly. Three seats, unlimited contacts, projects, docs and invoices, plus workflows, email, tickets and API.
  • Business: $99 a month billed annually, or $129 billed monthly. Seven seats, adds subscriptions, QuickBooks or Xero, client portal branding and a custom domain.
  • Scale: $199 a month billed annually, or $249 billed monthly. Fifteen seats and unlimited AI workers.

Extra seats are $20 a month. The branded client portal add-on for Starter and Pro is $19 a month, or $15 billed annually. The free trial lasts 14 days with Business features and no credit card. Your payment provider sets its own fees.

Thrive has one plan: $10 a month or $100 a year, with everything included. The 7-day trial needs no card. Connecting email and calendar waits until you subscribe.

A worked example: a solo designer who wants workflows and client email needs Agiled's Pro plan, which comes to $708 a year billed annually. On Thrive, the same designer pays $100 a year. Agiled's Pro price does include three seats; Thrive's workspace has one owner.

Where Thrive falls short

  • No payment processing. Invoices show your payment instructions; clients pay outside Thrive and you record the payment.
  • No recurring invoices, payment schedules or automatic payment reminders.
  • Typed-name signatures only, one signer per document.
  • No date-based workflow triggers. Workflows react to events, with one trigger and up to 20 actions.
  • No team roles. Each workspace has one owner.
  • No task lists or project management boards beyond Kanban boards for records, and no support tickets or HR tools.
  • No accounting: no expenses, bank feeds, tax reports or QuickBooks and Xero sync.
  • No mobile app, no client logins and no custom portal domain. Thrive runs in the browser.

How to switch from Agiled to Thrive

Finish open projects in Agiled and start new ones in Thrive, so no client gets two sets of links halfway through.

  1. Export your contacts. In Agiled, go to Settings, then Data export, and download the zip. It has a CSV file for your contacts.
  2. Import them into Thrive. Under Clients, choose Import clients. Thrive matches your columns to its fields and shows a review step before anything is saved. Up to 500 rows per file.
  3. Rebuild your proposal and contract templates from Thrive's starters, and paste in your own wording.
  4. Add your services and packages to the service catalog, so proposals and invoices reuse the same prices.
  5. Write your payment instructions in Settings, under Invoices.
  6. Connect your Google or Microsoft email and calendar, so client emails link to records and booking pages know when you're busy.
  7. Set up one or two workflows, such as "proposal accepted, create the contract".

Questions

Is Thrive cheaper than Agiled?

For one person, yes. Thrive is $100 a year with everything included. Agiled's Starter plan is $24 a month billed annually, and workflows start on Pro at $59 a month billed annually, as of November 2026.

Can clients pay by card in Thrive?

Not inside Thrive. Invoices show your payment instructions, which can include your own payment link. Clients pay outside Thrive and you record the payment.

Can I import my clients from Agiled?

Yes. Export your workspace data from Agiled's settings, then use Import clients in Thrive with the contacts CSV. Thrive matches columns and lets you review rows first.

Does Thrive have a mobile app like Agiled?

No. Thrive runs in the browser on any device. Agiled has apps for phones and computers.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Thrive's trial lasts 7 days with no card. Agiled offers 14 days with Business features, also with no card.
