Choose Plutio if

  • You want clients to pay invoices by card through Stripe, PayPal or Square.
  • You bill retainers and want the client's card charged automatically each cycle.
  • You manage tasks, subtasks and timelines for each project, often with a team.
  • You want native apps for iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows.
  • You want the whole app white-labeled under your own domain.

Choose Thrive if

  • You work alone or with a partner and want one low price with no client limit.
  • Your clients pay by bank transfer or a payment link you already have.
  • You don't need task lists and prefer a lighter tool for proposals, contracts, invoices and time.
  • You want open-source software you could host yourself.
  • You want Claude or ChatGPT to work with your real clients and invoices.

Feature by feature

Everything about Plutio below comes from plutio.com and Plutio's help center, read in November 2026.

Clients and pipeline

Plutio has contacts and companies with custom fields, color tags and custom views. The Core plan allows up to 9 active clients (archived ones don't count); Pro and Max have no limit.

Thrive has a client list with pipeline stages, custom fields, saved views and a Kanban board. There is no cap on clients.

Both cover the basics. Past 9 active clients, Thrive costs less.

Proposals and packages

Plutio lets clients choose packages and add-ons before signing, can require a deposit when a proposal is approved, can include a contract in the proposal, and creates projects and invoices automatically once it is signed.

Thrive has eight proposal and contract starters. Pricing can include all services, let the client choose one package or several, or follow per-item rules with adjustable quantities. With project billing on, the deposit is invoiced the moment the client accepts.

This one is close: both let the client choose and both start billing on acceptance.

Contracts and signatures

Plutio clients can draw a signature, type their name or upload an image. Each signature records the name, IP address, browser and device, and the time. Contracts can have as many signers as you need.

Thrive contracts are signed with a typed name, and Thrive records the name, consent, version and time. There is no drawn signature and no third-party e-signature service.

Plutio is stronger here.

Invoices and payments

Plutio integrates with Stripe, Square and PayPal, and clients can also pay by bank transfer. It offers instalments, a currency per client or invoice, and tax settings. Subscriptions can send an invoice each cycle or, with Stripe or Square, charge the stored card automatically. Plutio can send up to three automatic reminders per overdue invoice. It takes no transaction fee; processors charge theirs.

Thrive invoices show the payment instructions you write, such as bank details or your own payment link. Clients pay outside Thrive and you record the payment. There are no recurring invoices and no automatic reminders, though overdue invoices appear in your daily or weekly summary email.

Plutio is clearly stronger for getting paid.

Email and scheduling

Plutio's inbox connects Gmail through Google, and its help center has a guide for IMAP/SMTP inboxes. Emails can be linked to a contact or project, and there are direct messages and channels. Schedulers sync your calendars, add Zoom or Google Meet links, can take payment before a booking, and can be embedded.

Thrive connects one Google or Microsoft account. Client emails show up on each client, and you send from a draft you review. Booking pages have their own durations, buffers and notice, check your calendar, and don't take payment.

Plutio does more here, with channels and paid bookings.

Forms

Plutio has a drag-and-drop builder with templates, file uploads you can restrict by size and type, and a link or embed code.

Thrive forms take 1 to 50 questions, including file uploads, with nine starters. An email answer can create the client and a project-name answer a planned project. Embed inline or as a popup, in your fonts and colors.

Automations

Plutio automations start when a record is created, updated or deleted, a form is submitted, a webhook arrives, or a date approaches or passes. Each has up to 20 steps. Plans include a monthly number of workflow actions: 500 on Core, 5,500 on Pro.

Thrive workflows start from one event, such as a proposal accepted or a payment recorded, and run up to 20 actions: create the contract, issue a deposit invoice, draft an email. There are no date-based triggers and no monthly action quota.

Plutio is stronger if you need date-based automations.

Client portal

Plutio clients accept an invite, set a password, and return at your workspace address or your own domain with white label. You choose per client whether they see tasks, files, invoices and messages. They can pay invoices and upload files.

A Thrive portal is one private link per client, with no password. Pending documents, forms and unpaid invoices come first. Clients can accept or sign, fill in forms, upload files and download PDFs.

Plutio's portal does more. Thrive's suits clients who don't want another login.

Projects, tasks and time

Plutio has tasks with subtasks and list, Kanban, table, timeline and calendar views, plus a wiki and team permissions. Its timer runs in the browser, desktop and mobile apps, and hours become invoices in a click.

Thrive projects hold documents, invoices, time and messages, with no task lists. Time has a timer and manual entries, and unbilled time becomes an invoice draft.

Plutio is far stronger for task and team work.

AI, apps and languages

Plutio has its own assistant, Plutio Pal, which takes voice commands to create invoices or draft contracts; plans include AI credits. It has apps for iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and Windows, and supports 25+ languages.

Thrive is an MCP server: connect Claude or ChatGPT and the assistant can read your clients, draft a proposal or preview an import, with nothing sent until you confirm. Thrive runs in the browser, in 10 languages. Its code is open source (MIT), so you can also host it yourself.

Pricing

As of November 2026, Plutio lists three plans, billed monthly or yearly:

  • Core: $19 a month or $190 a year. Solo use, up to 9 active clients.
  • Pro: $49 a month or $490 a year. Unlimited clients, 30 contributors.
  • Max: $199 a month or $1,990 a year. Unlimited contributors (fair use), white label and single sign-on.

White label is $9 a month extra on Core and Pro. The trial is 7 days with no credit card.

Thrive has one plan: $10 a month or $100 a year, everything included. The trial is 7 days without a card; connecting email and calendar waits until you subscribe.

A solo designer with 15 active clients needs Plutio Pro: $490 a year. On Thrive, $100.

Where Thrive falls short

  • No payment processing. Clients pay outside Thrive and you record the payment.
  • No recurring invoices or automatic card charging.
  • No automatic payment reminders.
  • No date-based workflow triggers. One event trigger, up to 20 actions.
  • Typed-name signatures only.
  • No task lists or timelines; Kanban boards are for records.
  • No team roles, chat channels, wiki or white label.
  • No mobile or desktop app.
  • No accounting: no expenses, bank feeds or tax reports.

How to switch from Plutio to Thrive

  1. Export your contacts. In Plutio, open Contacts, click Import/Export, then Export as CSV. Clear filters first.
  2. Import them. In Thrive, Clients → Import clients: match columns, review rows, then save. Up to 500 rows per file.
  3. Add your services and packages to the service catalog.
  4. Rebuild proposal and contract templates from Thrive's starters with your own wording.
  5. Write payment instructions in Settings → Invoices.
  6. Connect Google or Microsoft email and calendar.
  7. Add a workflow or two, such as: when a proposal is accepted, create the contract.
  8. Finish open projects in Plutio and start new clients in Thrive.

Questions

Is Thrive cheaper than Plutio?

Yes. Thrive is $10 a month or $100 a year. Plutio starts at $19 a month or $190 a year for up to 9 active clients (as of November 2026).

Can clients pay by card in Thrive?

Not inside Thrive. Invoices can include a payment link from a service you already use, and you record the payment.

Can I import my clients from Plutio?

Yes. Export contacts from Plutio as CSV, then use Clients → Import clients in Thrive.

Does Thrive have task management?

No. There are Kanban boards for clients, projects and other records, but no task lists or subtasks.

Is there a free trial?

Both offer 7 days without a credit card.

Does Thrive have a mobile app?

No, it runs in the browser. Plutio has native apps.
