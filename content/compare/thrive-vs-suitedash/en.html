Choose SuiteDash if

  • You want clients to pay by card or bank transfer inside the app, with subscriptions and stored payment methods.
  • You need recurring invoices and automatic payment reminders.
  • You run a team and want unlimited staff, tasks, support tickets and courses in one place.
  • A fully white-labeled portal on your own domain, installable on clients' phones, matters to your brand.

Choose Thrive if

  • You're a freelancer or a small studio, and your clients pay by bank transfer or your own payment link.
  • You want proposals, contracts, invoices, time and client email working together without weeks of setup.
  • You want one plan with everything included for $10 a month or $100 a year.
  • You want open-source software you can self-host, that also works with Claude or ChatGPT.

Feature by feature

A naming note first: SuiteDash's middle plan is also called Thrive. When we mean that plan, we say "SuiteDash's Thrive plan".

Clients and pipeline

SuiteDash's CRM handles contacts and companies, with unlimited custom fields and roles for leads, prospects and clients. Every plan includes unlimited CRM contacts, staff and portals. Deal stage pipelines are listed from SuiteDash's Thrive plan.

Thrive's client list shows pipeline stages, and clients, projects, documents and invoices can be viewed as Kanban boards. There's no limit on how many clients you work with.

SuiteDash goes further for teams. For a freelancer's client list, both do the job.

Proposals and packages

SuiteDash proposals are online, multi-page documents that clients can view without logging in. A proposal can offer items, add-ons or packages for the client to choose from, and acceptance can create the invoice. The Dynamic Proposals Toolkit is listed from SuiteDash's Thrive plan.

Thrive proposals start from eight editable templates. You can include all services, let the client choose one package, or choose several, with quantities they can adjust. If project billing is on, the deposit invoice is issued as soon as the client accepts or signs. See how to offer packages in proposals.

Both handle package choices well. In Thrive they're part of the one plan.

Contracts and signatures

SuiteDash's help center describes digital signing with several signers per document, a signing order, and signers who type their name or draw their signature. It keeps an audit trail, and its website mentions IP tracking.

Thrive contracts are signed with a typed name. There are no drawn signatures, no multiple signers and no third-party e-signature service.

SuiteDash is stronger here, especially if contracts need more than one signer.

Invoices and payments

SuiteDash connects to Stripe, Braintree, Authorize.net, PayPal and Square, and its help center says it takes no cut of the payments your clients make. It offers subscriptions, stored payment methods, recurring invoice generators, and reminder emails for due and overdue invoices.

Thrive doesn't process payments. Your invoices show the payment instructions you write, such as bank details or your own payment link. Clients pay outside Thrive and you record the payment. There are no recurring invoices and no automatic payment reminders.

SuiteDash is clearly stronger for getting paid.

Scheduling

SuiteDash has appointment types for free or paid bookings, Google Calendar sync, website embeds, and meeting links through Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft.

Thrive has booking pages that check your connected Google or Microsoft calendar, with buffers and notice times. Paid bookings aren't possible. See booking client calls without the back-and-forth.

SuiteDash does more, especially if you charge for sessions.

Email

SuiteDash includes secure messaging with clients, live chat, and email marketing campaigns on every plan, with drip autoresponders from SuiteDash's Thrive plan.

Thrive connects your own Gmail or Outlook account. Conversations are linked to the client and project, and you reply from Thrive. Thrive doesn't send marketing campaigns.

SuiteDash for newsletters, Thrive for everyday client email.

Automations

SuiteDash's no-code automations react to events across the platform, such as a signed contract or a paid invoice, and can be chained. FLOWs guide new clients through forms, files and signatures. The pricing page lists the complete automation toolkit and FLOWs on the Pinnacle plan.

Thrive workflows pair one event with up to 20 actions: "when a proposal is accepted, create the contract and draft a welcome email." They're included in the one plan. They react to events only, not to dates.

SuiteDash's automations go deeper. Thrive's are quicker to set up and easier to read later.

Client portal

SuiteDash's portal is its centerpiece: a white-labeled space on your own domain, with your logo, colors and login page, branded emails, file sharing, messaging, and an app clients can install on their phone's home screen.

Thrive's client portal is a private link, with no login, showing the documents, forms, invoices, meetings and project status you choose to share. There are no client accounts, no portal messaging and no custom domain.

SuiteDash is stronger if the portal is part of what you sell.

Projects, tasks and time

SuiteDash includes task templates, Kanban boards, task timers, task dependencies (on Pinnacle), expense tracking, and timers you can add to invoices.

Thrive projects hold documents, invoices, time and messages, with Kanban boards for records but no task lists. Time entries by project turn into invoice drafts. Thrive doesn't track expenses.

SuiteDash is stronger if you manage tasks and teams in the same tool.

AI, languages and your data

SuiteDash has AI writing tools, AI agents, and an MCP connection for Claude and ChatGPT. According to its help center, these need its A.I. Service subscription or your own AI provider connection, and a new trial includes a free monthly tier. Its translations tool can show staff and clients different languages.

Thrive is also an MCP server. Claude or ChatGPT can look up clients, prepare proposals or invoices, and draft emails, with nothing sent until you approve. That's included in the plan. The app is available in 10 languages. Thrive is open source under the MIT license, so you can read the code or self-host it on your own server.

Both work with AI assistants. In Thrive, that connection is part of the plan.

Pricing

As of November 2026, SuiteDash's pricing page lists three plans, priced per account, not per user, with unlimited clients and staff:

  • Start: $19 a month or $180 a year. CRM, portals, white labeling, email campaigns, 100 GB storage.
  • Thrive: $49 a month or $480 a year. Adds drip autoresponders, the Dynamic Proposals Toolkit, deal pipelines and client live chat.
  • Pinnacle: $99 a month or $960 a year. Adds the complete automation toolkit, FLOWs onboarding, courses, support tickets and task dependencies.

There are also one-off "Century" payments and a SuiteDreams package ($1,499 plus $49 a month) with every Power-Up. The free trial lasts 30 days on Pinnacle, with no credit card. SuiteDash says it takes no cut of your clients' payments; your payment provider sets its own fees.

Thrive has one plan: $10 a month or $100 a year, with everything included. The 7-day trial needs no card. Connecting email and calendar waits until you subscribe.

A worked example: a solo consultant who wants SuiteDash's full automation toolkit is on Pinnacle at $960 a year. One who only needs deal pipelines and package proposals is on SuiteDash's Thrive plan at $480 a year. On Thrive, either one pays $100 a year. Keep in mind that SuiteDash's price covers a whole team, while Thrive's workspace has one owner.

Where Thrive falls short

  • No payment processing. Invoices show your payment instructions; clients pay outside Thrive and you record the payment.
  • No recurring invoices, subscriptions or automatic payment reminders.
  • Typed-name signatures only, one signer per document.
  • No date-based workflow triggers. Workflows react to events, with one trigger and up to 20 actions.
  • No team roles. Each workspace has one owner.
  • No task lists or project management boards beyond Kanban boards for records, and no support tickets, courses or communities.
  • No accounting: no expenses, bank feeds or tax reports.
  • No email marketing, no custom portal domain, and no mobile app. Thrive runs in the browser.

How to switch from SuiteDash to Thrive

Finish open projects in SuiteDash and start new ones in Thrive, so no client gets two sets of links halfway through.

  1. Export your contacts. In SuiteDash, go to CRM, then Contacts, and use Export to download a CSV with the fields you pick.
  2. Import them into Thrive. Under Clients, choose Import clients. Thrive matches your columns to its fields and shows a review step before anything is saved. Up to 500 rows per file.
  3. Rebuild your proposal and contract templates from Thrive's starters, and paste in your own wording.
  4. Add your services and packages to the service catalog, so proposals and invoices reuse the same prices.
  5. Write your payment instructions in Settings, under Invoices.
  6. Connect your Google or Microsoft email and calendar, so client emails link to records and booking pages know when you're busy.
  7. Set up one or two workflows, such as "proposal accepted, create the contract".

Questions

Is Thrive cheaper than SuiteDash?

For one person, yes. Thrive is $100 a year with everything included. SuiteDash's plans are $180, $480 and $960 a year as of November 2026, though each covers unlimited staff.

Can clients pay by card in Thrive?

Not inside Thrive. Invoices show your payment instructions, which can include your own payment link. Clients pay outside Thrive and you record the payment.

Can I import my clients from SuiteDash?

Yes. Export your contacts to CSV from SuiteDash's CRM, then use Import clients in Thrive, which matches columns and lets you review rows first.

Does Thrive have a white-label client portal like SuiteDash?

Thrive has client portals with your logo, shared by private link, but no custom domain, client logins or portal messaging.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Thrive's trial lasts 7 days with no card. SuiteDash offers 30 days on its Pinnacle plan, also with no card.
