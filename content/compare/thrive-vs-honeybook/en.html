Choose HoneyBook if

  • You want clients to pay by card, ACH, Apple Pay or Google Pay inside the same link that holds your proposal and contract.
  • You rely on autopay, payment plans, recurring invoices and automatic payment reminders.
  • You run your business from your phone and want an iOS or Android app.
  • You need automations that wait a few days or react to project dates.
  • You're based in the U.S., Canada, the UK or Australia, the countries HoneyBook supports.

Choose Thrive if

  • Your clients mostly pay by bank transfer, or through a payment link you already have.
  • You work from a country HoneyBook doesn't support yet, or in a language other than English.
  • You bill some work by the hour and want a timer that turns into invoice drafts.
  • You want one plan with everything at $10 a month, including workflows and booking pages.
  • You want open-source software you can self-host, and that works with Claude or ChatGPT.

Feature by feature

Clients and pipeline

HoneyBook's CRM keeps contacts, conversations, files, notes and payments in one client record, with a customizable pipeline, tags and custom fields on every plan.

Thrive has a client list with pipeline stages, custom fields (text, number, currency, date, choice, email, URL and more), saved views, and a Kanban board you can drag cards across.

Both cover the basics well.

Proposals and packages

HoneyBook proposals let clients select services, sign the contract, pay the invoice and book a meeting from a single link. You can offer packages and add-ons, and the invoice and contract update with whatever the client picks.

A Thrive proposal can show a pricing table where the client picks one package, several services or optional items, with quantities you allow them to change. Their choice and the agreed prices are recorded when they accept. With project billing on, the deposit invoice is created as soon as they accept or sign.

HoneyBook is stronger here because payment and scheduling sit inside the same file. If you only need the client to choose and accept, both do the job.

Contracts and signatures

HoneyBook contracts start from lawyer-vetted templates and use smart fields to fill in client details. Unsigned contracts can get automatic reminders. Clients can type or draw their signature, from any device.

In Thrive, the client signs a published contract by typing their name; Thrive records the name, consent, version and time. There's no drawn signature, no signer identity check and no third-party e-signature service.

HoneyBook is stronger for signatures.

Invoices and payments

This is the biggest difference. HoneyBook processes payments: cards, ACH bank transfers, Apple Pay and Google Pay, with payment plans, autopay, recurring invoices, late fees, tipping and automatic reminders for upcoming or overdue payments.

Thrive doesn't process payments. Each invoice shows the payment instructions you write once in Settings, such as your bank details or your own payment link. Clients pay outside Thrive, and you record the payment, which updates the balance they see. Thrive has no recurring invoices and no automatic payment reminders.

HoneyBook is clearly stronger if you want to be paid by card inside the tool.

Scheduling

HoneyBook's scheduler syncs with Google, Outlook and other calendars and offers buffers, intake forms, paid sessions, round-robin for teams and email or SMS reminders. Starter includes one session type; Essentials and Premium include unlimited session types.

Thrive has meeting types with your hours, time zone, notice and buffers. Once you connect a Google or Microsoft calendar, each meeting type can publish a booking page that checks your real availability, bookable up to 30 days ahead. Bookings land in Thrive and are matched to the client by email.

HoneyBook offers more scheduling options.

Email

HoneyBook connects your email so you can write to clients from inside a project, with scheduled sends, read receipts and AI-written drafts.

Thrive connects one Google or Microsoft mailbox. The Emails page pulls in recent conversations with your clients and links them to client records and projects. You review every email before it goes out.

Automations

HoneyBook automations start with one trigger, such as a new inquiry, a signed contract, a payment or a project date, then add actions, waits and conditions. An AI builder can draft an automation from a plain description. Automations come with Essentials and Premium, not Starter.

Thrive workflows pair one event with up to 20 actions: "when a proposal is accepted, create the contract and change the project status". They're included in the one plan. Workflows react to events only. There are no waits, conditions or date-based triggers, and email actions prepare drafts for you to review.

HoneyBook's automations are more powerful. Thrive's are simpler and come with the base price.

Client portal

HoneyBook includes a client portal on every plan, a dedicated page where clients find their files and project details. Removing the "Powered by HoneyBook" label from emails, forms and the portal starts on Essentials.

In Thrive you pick exactly which documents, forms, invoices, projects and meetings each client sees. From one link, they can accept or sign documents, fill in forms, download invoices and see payment instructions. Portals have no client logins, messaging or payments.

Time tracking

HoneyBook's plan comparison lists task management, task reminders and reports. Time tracking isn't among the features it lists.

Thrive has a timer and a time ledger by project. Billable entries become invoice drafts, grouped by entry, day or description. See time tracking for freelancers.

AI

HoneyBook AI is on every plan: email drafts, meeting notetaker, project recaps, follow-up suggestions and more. HoneyBook also works inside ChatGPT.

Thrive is an MCP server, so you can connect Claude or ChatGPT to your workspace and ask it to find a client, draft a proposal or prepare an invoice. Sending email always waits for your approval. See running your admin with AI.

Mobile

HoneyBook has an app for iOS and Android.

Thrive runs in the browser only, with no app.

Languages and regions

HoneyBook says it currently supports professionals in the U.S., Canada, the UK and Australia, with prices in USD, CAD, GBP and AUD.

Thrive is open to sign-ups from anywhere, and the app is in 10 languages: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese (Brazil), Polish, Turkish and Indonesian.

Your data and open source

HoneyBook is a hosted subscription. Its help center explains how to download your contacts as a CSV file.

Thrive's code is public under the MIT license. Use the hosted version or run it on your own server. Every list can be exported to CSV.

Pricing

As of November 2026, HoneyBook's pricing page lists three plans, billed yearly: Starter at $29/month, Essentials at $49/month and Premium at $109/month. Billing monthly costs more. Essentials adds the full scheduler, automations, QuickBooks Online and up to 2 team members; Premium adds unlimited team members. All plans start with a 30-day free trial without a credit card, and HoneyBook offers a 60-day money-back guarantee. Payments have their own processing fees, listed on the pricing page, with ACH bank transfers at 1.5%.

Thrive costs $10/month or $100/year. There's one plan, and everything is in it: workflows, booking pages, forms, client portals, time tracking and the AI connection. The free trial lasts 7 days, without a card. Connecting your email and calendar waits until you subscribe.

A worked example: a solo designer who wants automations needs HoneyBook Essentials, which is $588 a year billed yearly, before any processing fees. On Thrive, the same designer pays $100 a year and collects payments by bank transfer or their own payment link.

Where Thrive falls short

  • No payment processing. No cards, ACH, Apple Pay or Google Pay inside Thrive, and so no autopay, tipping or instant deposit.
  • No recurring invoices, autopay or automatic payment reminders. You send reminders yourself.
  • Typed-name signatures only. HoneyBook lets clients draw their signature.
  • No date-based workflow triggers, waits or conditions. Thrive workflows react to events.
  • No mobile app. Thrive runs in your browser.
  • No accounting. HoneyBook Finance lists expense tracking, a tax hub and financial reports; Thrive has none of those.
  • No task lists or project boards beyond Kanban boards for records, and no paid bookings or SMS reminders.

How to switch from HoneyBook to Thrive

Finish open projects in HoneyBook, especially those on autopay, and start new clients in Thrive.

  1. Export your contacts. In HoneyBook, go to Clients, then Contacts, open the three-dot menu and choose Download spreadsheet. You get a CSV file.
  2. Import them into Thrive. Go to Clients, then Import clients. Thrive matches your columns to its fields and shows a review step where you fix rows before anything is saved. Up to 500 rows per file.
  3. Rebuild your templates. Start from Thrive's proposal and contract starters and paste in your own wording.
  4. Add your services and packages to the service catalog, with fixed fees or hourly, daily, weekly or monthly rates.
  5. Write your payment instructions in Settings → Invoices. They appear on every invoice.
  6. Connect your Google or Microsoft email and calendar, so client emails link to their records and booking pages show your real availability.
  7. Set up one or two workflows, such as "when a proposal is accepted, create the contract". Our workflow ideas help.

Questions

Is Thrive cheaper than HoneyBook?

Yes. Thrive is $10/month or $100/year with everything included. HoneyBook starts at $29/month billed yearly, and automations start on Essentials at $49/month billed yearly, as of November 2026.

Can clients pay by card in Thrive?

Not inside Thrive. Your invoices show the payment instructions you write, which can include your own card payment link. Clients pay outside Thrive and you record the payment.

Can I import my clients from HoneyBook?

Yes. Download your contacts from HoneyBook as a CSV, then use Clients → Import clients in Thrive. You match columns and review rows before saving, up to 500 rows per file.

Is there a free trial?

Thrive has a 7-day free trial without a card. HoneyBook offers a 30-day free trial without a credit card.

Can I use Thrive outside the U.S., Canada, the UK and Australia?

Yes. Thrive has open sign-up, lets you choose your country and currency, and is available in 10 languages.
