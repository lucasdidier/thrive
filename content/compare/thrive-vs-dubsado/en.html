Choose Dubsado if

  • You want clients to pay by card, ACH, Klarna or Affirm, with payment plans, autopay and reminders that send themselves.
  • Your process depends on timed steps, like "two days before the session" or "a week after the project ends".
  • You want one link where a client reviews a proposal, signs the contract and pays.
  • You take payments in person or work from your phone, with Tap to Pay in the mobile app.
  • You need tasks, task boards and connections to QuickBooks, Xero or Zapier.

Choose Thrive if

  • Your clients mostly pay by bank transfer, or through a payment link you already have.
  • You want to be set up in an afternoon, with workflows that read like one sentence.
  • You want everything, including workflows, booking pages and time tracking, in one $10 plan.
  • You'd like the app in German, French, Spanish, Dutch or another of its 10 languages.
  • You want open-source software you can self-host, and that works with Claude or ChatGPT.

Feature by feature

Proposals and packages

Dubsado proposals combine a proposal, contract and invoice in one link, so a client can review, select, sign and pay in a single flow. You can show packages, add-ons and multiple selections. On Premier, public proposals can be embedded on your website to capture leads and start workflows.

A Thrive proposal can show a pricing table where the client picks one package, several services or optional items, then accepts or signs. Their choice and the agreed prices are recorded. With project billing on, the deposit invoice is created as soon as they accept or sign.

Dubsado is stronger here, because payment is part of the same link.

Contracts and signatures

Dubsado contracts use templates with smart fields and variable pricing tables. Its help center says signatures are typed digital signatures, and you can countersign after the client signs.

Thrive contracts are also signed by typing a name. Thrive records the name, consent, the exact published version and the time. There's no signer identity check and no third-party e-signature service.

The signing itself is similar; Dubsado adds countersigning.

Invoices and payments

Dubsado Payments, powered by Stripe, takes cards, ACH bank transfers in the U.S. and Canada, Klarna and Affirm, and Tap to Pay in person. PayPal and Square can also be connected. You get payment plans, recurring invoices, autopay, optional card surcharging and automatic payment reminders. To receive money through Dubsado Payments, your home country must be on its supported list (34 countries in its help center).

Thrive doesn't process payments. Each invoice shows the payment instructions you write once in Settings, such as bank details or your own payment link. Clients pay outside Thrive and you record the payment. There are no recurring invoices and no automatic payment reminders.

Dubsado is clearly stronger if you want to be paid inside the tool.

Forms

Dubsado has lead capture forms you can embed on your website, plus questionnaires and other forms. Starter allows one active lead capture form; Premier allows unlimited.

Thrive forms have 1 to 50 questions, from short text to ratings and file uploads, and nine starters such as project inquiry, client onboarding and creative brief. Embed any form on your website as a popup or inline, with fonts and colors to match your site.

Automations

Dubsado's workflows, called Flows, are on Premier. They react to client actions like a submitted form, a signed contract or a paid invoice, and also to time: a delay after the flow starts, a fixed date, or a date relative to the project or an appointment. Actions include sending emails, forms, contracts and invoices, creating tasks and activating the portal. Sending steps can wait for your manual approval.

Thrive workflows pair one event with up to 20 actions, such as "when a contract is signed, create the project, create the deposit invoice and add a tag". They're included in the one plan. Workflows react to events only, with no delays or date-based triggers, and email actions prepare drafts for you to review.

Dubsado's Flows do much more. Thrive's take minutes to set up and are easy to read later.

Scheduling

Dubsado's scheduler, on Premier, syncs both ways with Google and Outlook calendars, adds Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams links, and supports buffers, attached forms, reminders and payment to book.

Thrive has meeting types with your hours, time zone, notice and buffers. Once you connect a Google or Microsoft calendar, each meeting type can publish a booking page that checks your real availability, up to 30 days ahead. Bookings land in Thrive, matched to the client by email.

Dubsado offers more scheduling options.

Email

Dubsado connects Gmail, Microsoft 365 or another SMTP account, so you send from your own address. It tracks opens, and Premier adds scheduled sending.

Thrive connects one Google or Microsoft mailbox, pulls in recent conversations with your clients and links them to clients and projects. You review every email before it goes out.

Client portal

Dubsado's portal is branded, with an option to password-protect it and connect your own domain. Clients see forms, documents, appointments, project progress and payment history.

In Thrive you choose exactly which documents, forms, invoices, projects and meetings each client sees. From one link, they accept or sign, fill in forms, download invoices and see payment instructions. There are no client logins, messaging or payments in the portal.

Time tracking

Dubsado's Premier plan includes a time tracker with a timer, and you can apply entries to an invoice. Once applied, an entry becomes read-only.

Thrive has a timer and time entries by project on every subscription. Billable entries become invoice drafts, grouped by entry, day or description. See time tracking for freelancers.

Both do this well; on Thrive it isn't tied to a higher plan.

AI

Dubsado lists AI email thread summaries and a meeting notetaker that records calls and writes transcripts and summaries.

Thrive is an MCP server: connect Claude or ChatGPT and ask it to find a client, draft a proposal or prepare an invoice. Sending email always waits for your approval. See running your admin with AI.

Mobile

Dubsado has a free companion app for iOS and Android, with invoicing, leads, appointments, tasks and Tap to Pay.

Thrive runs in the browser only, with no app.

Your data and open source

Dubsado is a hosted subscription. Its help center explains how to export all your contacts as a CSV file.

Thrive's code is public under the MIT license. Use the hosted version or run it on your own server. Every list can be exported to CSV.

Pricing

As of November 2026, Dubsado's pricing page lists Starter at $335/year and Premier at $525/year, and says annual billing saves about two months. Its official product information lists the monthly prices as $35 for Starter and $55 for Premier. Workflows, the scheduler, public proposals, unlimited lead forms, time tracking, Zapier and QuickBooks are on Premier. Three additional users are free; more users and extra brands cost extra. The free trial lasts 21 days with Premier features and no credit card. Dubsado Payments has processing fees listed in its help center (for U.S. cards, 2.9% + 30¢).

Thrive costs $10/month or $100/year. There's one plan, with workflows, booking pages, forms, client portals, time tracking and the AI connection. The free trial lasts 7 days, without a card. Connecting email and calendar waits until you subscribe.

A worked example: a solo photographer who wants workflows and a scheduler needs Dubsado Premier, which is $525 a year billed yearly, or $660 a year paying monthly, before processing fees. On Thrive, the same photographer pays $100 a year.

Where Thrive falls short

  • No payment processing. No cards, ACH, Klarna, Affirm, PayPal, Square or Tap to Pay.
  • No recurring invoices, autopay or automatic payment reminders. You follow up yourself.
  • No date-based workflow triggers or delays. Nothing like "three days before the appointment".
  • Typed-name signatures only, and no countersigning flow like Dubsado's.
  • No mobile app. Thrive runs in your browser.
  • No task lists or project boards beyond Kanban boards for records.
  • No accounting: no expenses, bank feeds, tax reports or QuickBooks and Xero sync.

How to switch from Dubsado to Thrive

Let current projects finish in Dubsado, especially those on payment plans, and start new ones in Thrive.

  1. Export your contacts. In Dubsado, go to Contacts and click Export CSV, the download icon above the list. Choose the fields to include, then Export.
  2. Import them into Thrive. Go to Clients, then Import clients. Thrive matches your columns to its fields and lets you review and fix rows before saving. Up to 500 rows per file.
  3. Rebuild your forms and templates. Recreate your inquiry form from a Thrive form starter and embed it on your website. Paste your proposal and contract wording into Thrive's starters.
  4. Add your services and packages to the service catalog.
  5. Write your payment instructions in Settings → Invoices.
  6. Connect your Google or Microsoft email and calendar, so client emails link to their records and booking pages show your real availability.
  7. Recreate one or two key workflows, starting with "proposal accepted, create the contract". See our workflow ideas.

Questions

Is Thrive cheaper than Dubsado?

Yes. Thrive is $10/month or $100/year with everything included. As of November 2026, Dubsado Starter is $335/year and Premier, which has workflows and the scheduler, is $525/year.

Can clients pay by card in Thrive?

Not inside Thrive. Your invoices show the payment instructions you write, which can include your own card payment link. Clients pay outside Thrive and you record the payment.

Can I import my clients from Dubsado?

Yes. Export your contacts from Dubsado as a CSV, then use Clients → Import clients in Thrive, with column matching and a review step. Up to 500 rows per file.

Is there a free trial?

Thrive has a 7-day free trial without a card. Dubsado's trial lasts 21 days with Premier features and no credit card.

Can Thrive workflows send a reminder before a date?

No. Thrive workflows react to events, like a form submitted or a contract signed. Dubsado Flows can run actions relative to project and appointment dates.
